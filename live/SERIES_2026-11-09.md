## Anomalies

1. **A shell slip, no tx.** One batch ran under zsh, which doesn't word-split, so `topup` got no arguments. Forge refused at encoding ("encode length mismatch") before sending anything. The stray re-trace was stopped before it wrote a record. T3 was re-run on its own; T3–T5 started 3 min later than planned.
2. **`agent_retry.py` logged a successful pay as "ask failed" (D1, 07:01:59).** Forge sent `0x1a2585…d38f` (agent nonce 16) but timed out waiting for its receipt; the tx was mined 37 s later and **paid $1**. The retry loop then read unspent = 2 before that block, logged "served", and the final pay resolved to the same tx. The agent's nonce (33 at close) and `paid-sum` (12 payments, $17) show one payment, not two, so nothing was over-spent. A pay that is still pending could still be duplicated within the allowance, though the wallet's cap still bounds it. Fix before the next run: on a forge failure, check the agent's nonce or the last broadcast hash before counting the attempt as failed.
3. **The `stopcycle` `cast logs` on Base printed nothing**, so only Unichain's lines appeared. The cause is block time: Base Sepolia ran at ≈ 0.2 s per block today (53,735 blocks between 05:17 and 08:16 UTC). "The last 150 blocks" therefore covered only ≈ 30 s on Base, and after the 40-s sleep the ack was older than that. All 10 `EpochAck` on both chains were read back afterwards with chunked getLogs (table below). Fix: look back by time, not by a fixed block count.
4. **Reserves ran out at the end of the tail** (see lREACT). The run book's "don't deposit at the end" was kept.
5. Lasna react timestamps are often 1 s *before* the L2 origin's block time: the two chains' clocks differ by about 1 s, within the logger's 2-s skew. Every trace is linked by origin tx (`"origin-tx"`).
