## 2. Directed test of a 48 h debt pause

`test/Directed.t.sol`: `test_debtPause48h_stopOnChain0_agentOnChain1` (the reviewed case) and `test_debtPause48h_stopOnChain2_agentOnChain1` (the same with the press on chain 2). Both run at production timing: waits 30/30/16 min, heartbeat 40 min, Cron every 17.74 min.

**Scenario:**
- Day d0, 06:00: the chains get $8, $8 and $4, and spend $2, $3 and $1. The pool is empty and nothing is in flight.
- **The pause is 48 h in 5-min steps.** There is no Cron tick and no react. After every step every queued origin log is dropped, and the test asserts that no callback was ever queued. This is the worst case of the open question to Reactive: the events are dropped rather than queued.
  - The owner presses STOP on chain 0 two hours in. That chain freezes at once, and its agent then stays idle.
  - Chain 1's agent pays $0.25 every hour: 18 payments on d0 ($4.50 of its $5). It is refused from d0 + 1 on. Its Paid, LowWater and Short logs are all lost.
- **Resume:** exactly one `tick()`, then `deliverAll()`, with no second tick. That covers the tick, the heartbeat re-reads it sends to the two overdue chains, the pressing wallet's re-announced press, and the epoch messages.

**What it asserts:**
- **STOP within one react of the resume:** the coordinator is frozen, and every wallet is frozen on the coordinator's epoch, after that one tick's cascade. With the press on chain 2, the first tick beats only chains 0 and 1 (at most 2 per call). Chain 2's beat goes out on the next react, which is the answer to the first beat, not the next Cron tick. The test shows this.
- **Leftovers swept:** the day-d0 buckets are gone and nothing is unspent or stranded. Every wallet holds 0 tokens, and vault + paid = funded on every chain. Before the resume the test asserts that the pressing chain's and the idle chain's leftovers were still stuck.
- **No overspend:**
  - chain 1 paid exactly $4.50;
  - day d0 outflow = $10.50 in all;
  - $0 on d0 + 1, the day the coordinator never opened, and $0 on the resume day;
  - chain 1 is refused after STOP;
  - `safetyFailure()` passes: (a), (b), (e) and token backing.
- **Books conserve:** pool + Σ held = cap on d0 and on the resume day. d0 + 1 is never opened (cap 0).
- Then 2 h of production running and `healAndSettle()`: invariant (s), the owner's STOP still holds everywhere.

**Mutation check.** Each mutant ran in a scratch copy of the project, the way `script/mutants.sh` does it, so `src/` in the repo was never edited. `diff -r` with the control copy is empty, and `git status src` is clean.

The two ad hoc patches aren't added to `script/patch_mutants.py`, so the 39-mutant run is unchanged. Z1, Z2, M9 and M13 are already in it and now also die on these tests.

**A nuance the test surfaced (no code change):** a *refused* `pay()` sweeps yesterday's leftover into `stranded`, but only a successful pay flushes. So during the pause, chain 1's $0.50 sat in the wallet. It was owed to the vault and never spendable, and it went back on the first coordinator message after the resume. Anyone's `flush()` would also have returned it then, because `flush()` doesn't need a sweep once one has happened. Noted in the honest list of `docs/DESIGN_AND_TESTS.md`, next to the `flush()` item.
